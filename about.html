About me

My research focuses on mental health and psychological assessment in family, health and gender psychology, with a particular interest in attachment, trauma and the well-being of young adults, LGBTQ+ communities and older adults. I am also exploring clinical psychology to strengthen my theoretical and methodological foundations.

I studied psychology at Ho Chi Minh City University of Education (2022 – 2026). Since 2023 I have been part of the Psychological Research Laboratory, where I help design studies, prepare ethics applications and collect data. I have also worked as a research assistant at Thu Duc City Hospital and completed two clinical psychology internships.

I analyse data in SPSS. If our interests overlap, email is the best way to reach me.
